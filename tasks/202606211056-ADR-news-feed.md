# ADR: "News Feed" — narrative surface over the Momentum backend

- **Status:** Proposed (design agreed; not yet implemented)
- **Date:** 2026-06-21
- **Scope:** Adds a **News / Player Watch** feature: an editorial, chronological stream of session **previews** ("Can Chris continue his 4-trophy streak?") and **recaps** ("Jonathen broke his 3× wooden-spoon streak and finished 2nd"), generated from the existing Momentum backend plus rankings history. Display-only. No new data capture and no changes to the momentum maths.
- **Builds on:** [`202606091200-ADR-momentum-metric.md`](./202606091200-ADR-momentum-metric.md). Momentum is the **engine**; News Feed is a **presentation/derivation layer** on top of it. Deliberately named separately so the two evolve independently.

---

## Context

The Momentum ("Form") boards shipped as a display-only pilot: a signed, self-relative heat value on the Champions Hall (placement substrate) and Ballers Board (contribution substrate), sorted hottest-first as leaderboards.

Monitoring the first live session (2026-06-20) surfaced not a defect but a **presentation problem**, via two recurring "that looks wrong" moments that were both the metric working as designed:

- **Ballers Board** — four players from the day's most-beaten team (blue hailstorms) landed in the top 8. Cause: the substrate is individual contribution _volume_ vs the player's **own** baseline (not team result — that's the other board), amplified for very consistent players because the `k = own MAD` normaliser collapses toward ~1 on a tight history (Tinashe: MAD 1.0 on 5 sessions). Accurate, but counterintuitive at a glance.
- **Champions Hall** — Kat won the league and reached the cup final yet read **cold** (−0.18). Cause: the substrate is normalised _placement_ vs his own (high) baseline, weighted league:cup ≈ 3:1; his recent cup wins came from low league finishes, so his trophy-streak badge and his heat value legitimately diverged. The −0.18 was a deep recovery (−0.996 → −0.18 in one session) that hadn't yet crossed zero.

Both cases share one failure mode: **a single signed scalar, read as a ranking, contradicts the story the viewer already has — and reconciling it takes a paragraph about EMAs, MAD and weighting.** That is the signature of "another confusing number," not drama.

The question therefore shifted from _"is momentum accurate?"_ (yes) to _"is it entertaining, or just noise?"_. The conclusion: the drama was always in the **trajectory** (the shape of the arc) and in the **streaks and swings** — never in the bare scalar level. The News Feed is the surface that presents those as legible stories.

---

## Decision

### 1. A narrative layer, not a new metric

News Feed is a **pure derivation** over (a) the momentum module's existing outputs — per-session `series` (the trajectory), `championsTrophyStreak`, `woodenSpoonStreak`, baller award badge streaks — and (b) the yearly rankings history. **No new data is captured and the momentum maths is untouched.** This ADR is entirely about _selection and presentation_.

### 2. Trajectory over snapshot

Present form as an **arc + narrative**, not a signed scalar. Rationale:

- A bare value (e.g. "Kat: −0.18, cold") throws away the one thing — the shape — that makes self-relative momentum dramatic, then reads as wrong because it's a position-on-a-journey being mistaken for a rank.
- The **series already encodes the journey**; a rising sparkline still slightly negative is literally "comeback brewing".

**Keep the own-baseline substrate; never league-baseline.** A "vs league average" framing would manufacture a caste system — strong players parked permanently hot, weak players structurally frozen cold, with movement only via "win/lose by more". That just rebuilds the elo ladder. Self-relative is exactly what lets a whipping-boy heat up (Tinashe) and a serial champion cool (Kat); those are the engine of drama, not bugs.

### 3. Format: chronological stream, computed on the fly — **not** a leaderboard

- A **ranked 1..N board** over a noisy scalar over-promises ordinal precision the metric can't honour ("why am I #8 not #6?" → "noise, and your MAD is 3") and re-imports the cross-player comparison that the low-MAD amplification makes unfair. **Retired** for the feed.
- The feed is a **chronological stream of self-contained cards**, newest first, with **load-more / infinite scroll**.
- A **movable "as-of" date clock** lets the stream be viewed as it stood at any past point.

### 4. Preview vs Recap is a per-card state, set by one boolean

For each session card the voice is chosen by: **are this session's results known as of the viewing clock?**

- **No** (the leading / upcoming session relative to the clock) → **Preview voice**: open questions. _"Can Chris continue his 4-trophy streak? Tinashe comes in red hot. Jonathen looks to end a 3-session spoon run."_
- **Yes** (a completed session) → **Recap voice**: resolutions. _"Chris makes it 5. Jonathen breaks the spoon run with a 2nd."_

Because the feed is computed on the fly, a card **flips Preview → Recap for free** once the session is played and the page is reloaded — no stored state to migrate. This is a direct benefit of the on-the-fly choice (§9).

### 5. Self-contained cards (no cross-card pairing)

The preview→recap _payoff_ is delivered **inside each recap card** by restating its own setup — _"Jonathen came in on a 3-session wooden-spoon streak — and snapped it with a 2nd-place finish."_ No card depends on an adjacent card being loaded, which is exactly what infinite scroll needs. Per-thread recap outcomes (all already implied by the streak logic, which skips unobserved sessions):

- **Extended** — _"…makes it 5."_
- **Broken** — _"…ends at 4."_
- **Started** — a new notable run begins.
- **Carried over** — player didn't feature: _"Chris sat out; his streak stays alive at 4."_

### 6. Anchoring: `board(D)` = form going **into** session D, frozen

A session's board is computed over sessions **strictly before** D, cooled to date D:

> **board(D) = momentum over sessions with date `< D`, with `now = D` (pinned).**

This is the **"form going in"** semantics, chosen over "post-ranking-update form" for **consistency**: session D's own result never enters its own board, so the card reads **identically before and after** the ranking update fires. Two bounds are both required — the strict `< D` filter removes the result-injection, and pinning `now = D` freezes the calendar cooling; pin only one and it still wobbles. The live/upcoming board is just the special case `D = next session date`. Reproducing "as of date T" is the same function with the clock at T — consistent with the on-the-fly stream (§3, §9).

**Accepted consequence:** a blockbuster session never appears on its _own_ board; it pays off in the **next** session's Preview. Tinashe going nuclear in session N makes him a "player to watch" for N+1. The feed is one beat behind the live action **by design** — which is the right rhythm for a "who to watch going in" surface (a big week earns next week's spotlight).

### 7. Year roll-over: fresh start, already the behaviour

Momentum — and therefore the feed — is **already year-scoped**: it is built solely from the current-year `rankings-YYYY.json` history, gated to `year === currentYear`. It does **not** carry across years like elo (whose rating is all-time, `eloGames.allTime`, and feeds team balancing). So **"new year, fresh start" needs no wiping** — a new year starts from an empty history and form re-accrues. The `damp = min(n / minSessions, 1)` term gives a natural early-season ramp (nobody is nuclear in week 2), reinforcing the clean-slate feel. **Deferred (cosmetic):** whether the empty week-1 feed shows nothing or a "fresh season — form builds from here" placeholder.

### 8. Content taxonomy (typed threads)

A small set of typed threads, each with a Preview and a Recap face, scored for notability so the page shows the **top ~5–8**, not everything:

**Streak threads** (headline-makers):

- Trophy streak ≥ 2 (`championsTrophyStreak`).
- Wooden-spoon streak ≥ 2 (`woodenSpoonStreak`).
- Baller award streak ≥ 2 (MVP / Golden Boot / Playmaker / Brick Wall / Golden Glove badges).

**Momentum threads** (swings):

- Red-hot / on-the-rise (high value or strong positive recent swing).
- Comeback brewing (recovering from deep negative but still cold — the Kat case).
- Biggest mover / faller (largest momentum swing this session).
- Breakout (low-baseline player posts a personal best).

**Plain news:** league / cup winners (the basic silverware line).

**Editorial selection is the point** (per the drama-vs-confusion conclusion): each candidate gets a **notability score** weighting streak length, swing magnitude, and **rarity** (breaking a long spoon run > extending a 2-streak). **Favour change/novelty over restating ongoing state** — decay threads that have been the same question for weeks so new/broken/swinging ones rise. This editorial layer is what keeps the feed a sports desk rather than a stat dump.

### 9. Generation & placement

- **Computed on the fly** from rankings; **not** pre-stored. Cheap here because `computeMomentum`'s `series` already yields the value at **every** session date from one pass per player — the whole season's stream comes from a single derivation, and "load more" just renders further into an already-computed structure (≈25 sessions × ≈60 players is negligible). Paginate client-side.
- **Templated headlines** to start (parameterised strings as above). LLM-generated prose **deferred** (cost / latency / unpredictability) until thread-selection is proven.
- **Home:** a dedicated **"News / Player Watch"** page (the chronological stream is its body). Plus a one-line **discovery teaser by the registration window** — _"3 stories to watch this week →"_ — because a page nobody navigates to is dead; the teaser supplies push without cluttering the main flow.
- The per-player **profile Form panel** (form value + streak + trajectory sparkline) is the canonical individual artifact that feed cards can link into. Feed = headline; profile = full arc.

## Consequences

**Positive / by design:**

- **Legible drama.** Stories (arc + narrative + streak) instead of a scalar that needs a paragraph to defend.
- **Anti-caste preserved** via own-baseline substrate; anyone can have an arc.
- **No new data** and **no momentum-maths change** — pure derivation.
- **Free Preview→Recap transition** from on-the-fly recompute.
- **Historical consistency** — `board(D)` is frozen, so a card reads the same before and after the ranking update, and the as-of clock reproduces any past moment.
- **Self-contained cards** suit infinite scroll with zero cross-card coupling.

**Negative / accepted trade-offs:**

- **One beat behind.** A session's own heroics surface in the _next_ preview, not its own card (§6) — accepted as the correct "going-in" rhythm.
- **Editorial selection is a new tuning surface.** Notability scoring + novelty-decay are knobs that need calibration against real weeks; a feed lives or dies on whether 3–4 genuinely good stories exist most weeks.
- **Narrative templates need maintenance** and must cover the carried-over / no-data cases gracefully.
- **The momentum scalar's known quirks still exist** (low-MAD amplification; minSessions knife-edge; league:cup placement weighting). The feed **reframes** them — surfacing streaks/swings, which are more legible than the raw value — rather than fixing them. Deeper fixes remain **deferred to the momentum feature** (see below).

---

## Rejected / Deferred

- **Ranked leaderboard format for the feed (rejected).** Over-promises ordinal precision the metric can't honour and re-imports the unfair cross-player sort (low-MAD player out-ranks a volatile one on a noisy column). Story-stream instead.
- **League-baseline / "vs league average" normalisation (rejected).** Rebuilds the caste system (win/lose-by-more); keep own-baseline. Consistent with the momentum ADR's own-baseline decision.
- **Real-time / live feed during a session (rejected).** Momentum only updates at the end-of-session ranking recalc — the data is **never live**. Embrace the weekly pre-match-preview cadence; it suits the genre (form guide / matchday preview) better than a live ticker would.
- **Post-ranking-update board semantics (rejected)** in favour of the frozen "going-in" `board(D)` (§6), for pre/post-session consistency.
- **Cross-card Preview/Recap pairing (rejected).** Self-contained cards (§5) — the recap restates its own setup, so infinite scroll has no card-to-card dependency.
- **Pre-stored per-session feed artifact (deferred).** On-the-fly chosen for simplicity and the free Preview→Recap flip; revisit only if compute ever becomes non-trivial.
- **LLM-generated narrative prose (deferred).** Templates first; add generated flavour later once thread-selection is validated.
- **Momentum-value fixes** (floor `k` so a collapsed MAD can't divide to ~1; soften the `minSessions` own-vs-league switch; revisit league:cup placement weighting) — **deferred to the momentum feature.** The News Feed reframes these rather than depends on them.
- **Empty week-1-of-year placeholder (deferred, cosmetic).**

## Appendix: implementation notes for LLD

**Data sources (all existing):**

- `buildChampionsMomentum` / `buildBallersMomentum` → per-player `value`, `series` (per-session trajectory), `components`, and streak/badge fields.
- `championsTrophyStreak`, `currentStreak`-derived `woodenSpoonStreak`, and the baller category badge streaks.
- `rankings-YYYY.json` history for placement (`performance`) and contribution (`stats`).

**As-of derivation (one call per board, per viewing clock `D`):** bound observations **and** the pooled aggregates (`leagueK` MAD pool, per-date award `tops`, derived team counts) to `date < D`; pass `now = D`. The strict `<` excludes session D's own result; everything else flows through `computeMomentum` unchanged. The `series` it returns is the per-session trajectory used to detect swings.

**Card model (sketch):** `{ sessionDate, state: 'preview' | 'recap', threads: Thread[] }` where each `Thread` is `{ type, subjectPlayer, magnitude/streakLength, outcome?: 'extended'|'broken'|'started'|'carriedOver', notability }`. Render the top ~5–8 threads across the session by `notability`.

**Notability (sketch):** monotone in streak length and swing magnitude, with a rarity bonus (breaking a long run) and a **novelty decay** for threads that have been the same open question over recent weeks. Tune against real distributions once a few weeks of feed exist — same empirical posture as the momentum ADR's `k`/half-life calibration.

**Swing detection:** compare the last two relevant points of a player's `series` (the structure already contains every session's value, so no extra computation).
